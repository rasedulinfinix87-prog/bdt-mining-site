Extreme Privacy & Protection

Last Updated: October 2026 • Goldmine Digital Standard

1. Commitment to Security

At Goldmine Digital, we prioritize the absolute confidentiality, integrity, and security of your financial digital assets and personal credentials. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is gathered, encrypted, stored, and protected when using our platform.

By accessing or utilizing Goldmine Digital investment services, you acknowledge and agree to the terms described in this extreme data protection policy.

2. Information We Collect

To facilitate secure investment processing and ensure Regulatory Compliance (KYC/AML), we collect the following datasets:

  • Identity Details: Full name, verified email address, phone number, and optional profile data.
  • Financial Records: Transaction IDs, deposit/withdrawal logs, subscription history, and digital gold asset holdings.
  • Technical Logs: IP addresses, browser specs, device identifiers, and login timestamp logs via Supabase Auth.

3. Bank-Grade Encryption

Your privacy is fortified through military-grade multi-layer architecture:

  • In-Transit Protection: End-to-end SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption for all server transactions.
  • Rest Data Encryption: Financial records & authentication tokens are stored with AES-256 standards.
  • Zero Password Exposure: Account passwords are hashed and unreadable even by internal administrators.

4. How We Use Your Data

We process data exclusively for core platform operations:

  • Executing gold purchases, subscriptions, and withdrawal payouts.
  • Preventing fraud, unauthorized access, and suspicious transactions.
  • Sending automated account receipts, profit notifications, and system updates.

5. Zero Third-Party Selling

Goldmine Digital never sells, rents, or trades user data to third-party advertisers or marketers. Data is shared strictly with essential partners under non-disclosure obligations, such as:

  • Payment gateway partners for processing transactions securely.
  • Law enforcement agencies, solely upon official, valid legal mandates or court warrants.

6. Investment Disclosure

Gold and digital assets carry market dynamics. While Goldmine Digital maintains strict internal controls and secure ledger tracking, users are advised to invest responsibly within their financial parameters.

7. User Data Rights

You retain absolute authority over your stored personal information:

  • Request full copies of your account records or transaction history logs.
  • Update or request correction of account holder details.
  • Request complete account termination and data purge (subject to legal financial auditing retention rules).

For questions regarding this policy or data safety inquiries, reach our dedicated compliance officer via Contact Us page.
